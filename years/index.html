    iPhone widgets come from the free Scriptable app, which draws these year dots on your home screen and updates them every day.

    2. then in Scriptable tap +, paste, and name it Years. (One time only.)
    3. On your home screen, long-press → + → Scriptable, and pick a small, medium or large widget.
